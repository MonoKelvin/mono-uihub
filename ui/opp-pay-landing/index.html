Lattice works with illustrators and motion designers in eleven countries. Before Oppopay, every invoice meant a wire form, a currency conversion and a week of follow-up to confirm the money had arrived.

Now each collaborator is paid through a local rail in their own currency. The invoice reference travels with the payment, so reconciliation happens when the money lands rather than at the end of the month.
