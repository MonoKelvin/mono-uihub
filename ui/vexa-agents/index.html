[ A FEW THINGS TO KNOW ]

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

What can I build here?

Choose an identity, give your agent a name and a role, then export its configuration. A connected workspace is required to publish and run it.

Can I change the agent's appearance?

Yes. The skin is separate from the core. Switch between Silver and Sienna without changing your agent's name or purpose.

Who decides what an agent can do?

You do. Define its role and review the action permissions in your connected workspace before allowing it to act.

Do I need a paid plan to start?

No. Build lets you create and export a configuration. Choose Scale when you want to connect a workspace; plan activation happens there.
