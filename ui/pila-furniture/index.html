BLOOEY / THE CLASSIC PILA

Something familiar.
Something lasting.

The Pila is a place to pause. An everyday object, reimagined through honest materials and a quiet architectural form.

A SIMPLE DAILY RITUAL

Stone. Water. Time.

A broad basin and a single, sculptural pedestal. The Classic Pila keeps the silhouette simple and lets the natural character of marble do the talking. Each piece of stone has its own pattern; no two surfaces are exactly alike.
