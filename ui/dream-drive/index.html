Got questions? We’ve got
answers!

Here are some of the most common questions about our
luxury car rental service.

How do I book a car?

Booking is simple and fast. Browse our fleet online, choose your preferred car, and complete your reservation with your personal details. You can also contact our team directly via phone or email for assistance.

Can the car be delivered to my location?

Yes. Choose hotel or airport delivery when planning your ride. Delivery is available across Dubai and Abu Dhabi, with the final fee confirmed by our team.

What is the minimum rental period?

Our minimum rental period is 24 hours. Weekly and monthly packages are available for longer stays.

Is insurance included in the rental price?

Standard insurance is included. An optional protection package can reduce your excess. Our team will explain the cover before your rental begins.

What documents do I need to rent a car?

Bring a valid driving licence, passport or Emirates ID, and a credit card. International visitors may also need an international driving permit.

Is there a security deposit required?

A refundable security deposit is required. The amount depends on your chosen vehicle and is shown in its details before you book.
